MATERIAL & DETAIL

Material Matters.

Fine millwork is defined by raw material selection: timber grain, mortise joinery, natural finishes, and hardware proportions.

Select Timber Hand-selected hardwoods evaluated for grain structure, stability, and natural finish response.
Precision Joinery Traditional mortise and tenon joints combined with modern engineered hardware.
Hardware Detail Unlacquered aged brass inlays, linear trim, and custom flush hardware details.
Natural Finishes Hardwax oils and matte finishes that preserve the raw tactile grain of the wood.

Our Process

TIMELINE

Discovery

Understanding the space, architectural drawings, scope, and design intent.

Design Development

Developing concept details, timber sample approvals, and joinery engineering drawings.

Material & Detail

Hardwood selection, grain matching, and joinery planning prior to workshop crafting.

04 / STEP

Fabrication

Precision workshop fabrication, hand joinery, and oil finishing.

05 / STEP

Installation

Careful white-glove site delivery, fitting, scribe alignment, and final detailing.
